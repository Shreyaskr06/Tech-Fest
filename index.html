Subhash Chandra Bose

Netaji : A Great Freedom Fighter of India

Subhash Chandra Bose was one of the most important leaders of India's freedom struggle. He is remembered for his courage, leadership and dedication towards the independence of India.

Early Life

Subhash Chandra Bose was born on 23 January 1897 in Cuttack, Odisha. His father's name was Janakinath Bose and his mother's name was Prabhavati Devi. He was a brilliant student and completed his education in India and England. He also passed the Indian Civil Services examination but decided to leave that career because he wanted to work for India's freedom.

Struggles

Bose faced many difficulties during his fight against British rule. He was arrested several times and spent many years in prison. He also had differences with some Congress leaders regarding the methods of achieving independence. Despite these challenges, he never gave up on his dream of a free India.

Contributions

His biggest contribution was leading the Indian National Army (INA), also known as Azad Hind Fauj. He wanted to free India from British rule through an organised military struggle. He inspired thousands of Indians to join the freedom movement. His famous slogans, "Jai Hind" and "Give me blood, and I will give you freedom", motivated many people.

Why Is He Famous?

Subhash Chandra Bose became famous because of his fearless leadership, patriotic spirit and contribution to India's independence movement. He is popularly known as "Netaji". His leadership of the Indian National Army made him one of the most respected figures in Indian history.
